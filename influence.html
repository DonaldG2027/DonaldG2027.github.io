Influence of Donville

Donville was inspired by some of the key themes of Isabel Allende's The House of the Spirits. For instance, the peasant community in the Tres Marias Estate in the novel showed how the influence of Estaban Trueba's reign affected the wellbeing of the estate and people around it, ultimately leading to it being left behind after Esteban sees the wrongs of his ways. Seeing how all of this played out made me realize the kind of vision I wanted when coming up with Donville, which in contrast, has a stable community with a strong sense of identity and purpose.

Another element from the novel that influenced Donville is the importance of family and community bonds in overcoming adversity. In The House of the Spirits, the Trueba family faces numerous challenges, including political upheaval and personal tragedies. However, their strong familial ties and support for one another help them navigate these difficulties. This theme of resilience and the power of community is reflected in Donville's emphasis on fostering a close-knit and supportive environment for its residents, like with the middle and high schools still standing despite the numerous challenges they have faced.
