Donville's Community

Within the community of Donville, there is always a place people can feel welcomed and they can comfortably call home. There are plenty of places around the community people can go to enjoy themselves, whether it be to view art, go shopping, eat, or hang out with friends, Donville has it.

Key Community Places

The Complex at Aiden Jolly

An arts hub named after benefactor Aiden Jolly, this complex is serves as an arts hub for the community to see all kinds of things.
Whether it be a gallery, a performance, or even a place to learn about the arts, this complex has it all.

The Mills at Cherry Gardens

Located in the heart of Cherry Gardens Township, The Mills is a vibrant commercial center that offers a wide range of services and amenities to the community.
From shopping and dining to entertainment and recreation, The Mills is a hub of activity that brings people together and fosters a sense of community.

Flyland Park

On the outskirts of Donville and not to far from the Colorado River, Flyland Park is a beautiful and expansive park that offers a wide range of recreational activities for visitors of all ages.
With its lush greenery, scenic walking trails, and well-maintained sports fields, Flyland Park is the perfect destination for outdoor enthusiasts and families looking to spend quality time together.
There's plenty of things to do, whether it be go for a walk, mess around on the playground, or even have a picnic, Flyland Park has it all.

Batcave & Breakfast

Made in honor of patron Hayden Frobenius (who is also the founder/key benefactor of Frobenius Manor) who passed before the establishment of this bed and breakfast,
Batcave & Breakfast offers a unique and memorable experience for guests looking for a cozy and comfortable place to stay.
Having a variety of Batman themed rooms and experiences throughout the establishment, this place is a go-to for not just Batman fans, but for anyone looking for a fun and unique place to stay.
