Residency

Donville has a handful of residency places available for those interested in our lovely town and would love to live here.

The Estates at Ebanks Hill

The Estates at Ebanks Hill is a residential development in Donville. Named after the historic Ebanks family, and sponsored by benefactor Gabriel Ebanks, it offers a unique living experience in the heart of the town. Townhomes are designed with modern amenities and a touch of historical charm, providing residents with a comfortable and stylish living environment. The development features landscaped gardens, walking paths, and community spaces that encourage social interaction and a sense of community among residents. The Estates at Ebanks Hill is conveniently located near local shops, restaurants, and schools, making it an ideal choice for families and individuals seeking a vibrant and welcoming neighborhood. With its blend of history, modern living, and community spirit, The Estates at Ebanks Hill is a sought-after destination for those looking to call Donville home.

Frobenius Manor:

Named after benefactor Hayden Frobenius, this residential place offers a luxurious living experience with its stunning architecture and beautiful surroundings.

With houses ranging from one to four bedrooms, Frobenius Manor provides a variety of options for families and individuals looking for a comfortable and stylish home.


The Oaks at Ventura Way

For those looking for something more laidback, The Oaks at Ventura Way offers a peaceful and serene living environment with its beautiful surroundings and tranquil atmosphere.
